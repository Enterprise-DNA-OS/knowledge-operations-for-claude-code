# Why no front end

The internal knowledge desk is articles, owners, revisions, reviews and reader corrections. A coding agent can read and change these records through one tested CLI. The same data produces branded read-only reports without a hosted application.

A hosted knowledge platform also gives readers a search page, authentication, accessible article layouts, media, public links and editing screens. This base does not provide those. It does not collect live readership statistics, enforce imported reader groups, offer mobile editing, support offline reader sync or provide drag-and-drop categorisation. Imported views and ratings are snapshots; searches and feedback are logged explicitly.

This is useful for a trusted operator managing an internal handbook. Enterprise DNA can build the reader experience and access controls around the same database, on the stack your business chooses. The agent is the operator's door, not a substitute for authenticated reader permissions.
