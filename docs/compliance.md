# Privacy evidence and house rules

Sources checked 21 October 2026. These are checks of recorded evidence, not a determination that a business meets every legal obligation. Applicable industry rules and Australian obligations require a separate assessment for that business.

| Rule | Source | What this system checks |
|---|---|---|
| NZ-IPP8 | [Privacy Commissioner, principle 8](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/8/) | Personal-information articles need a recorded accuracy check and an unexpired operator-chosen review date. |
| NZ-IPP9 | [Privacy Commissioner, principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) | All personal-information records, including archived articles, need a stated purpose and a future retention review. |

Principle 8 concerns reasonable checks before use or disclosure. A database date cannot establish factual accuracy. Principle 9 concerns retaining information only while needed for a lawful purpose. A future review date cannot justify retention by itself. The responsible person checks the purpose, applies other applicable retention duties and decides whether to remove or redact the records. This tool never deletes automatically.

## House rules

HOUSE-01 requires an active owner. HOUSE-02 requires independent review evidence for an active revision. HOUSE-03 requires an explicit privacy classification. These are operating controls chosen for this base, not statutory requirements.

The owner sets review and retention-review dates. No fixed number of days is claimed as a legal period. Revising clears approval and the previous accuracy-check marker. Activation requires an independent current-revision approval, future review date, active owner, known classification, resolved corrections, and for personal information, purpose, future retention review and accuracy-check marker. Historical imported articles remain visible with their gaps flagged.

Independent means different operator-supplied editor and reviewer labels. It is not authenticated separation of duties. Sign-in, enforced authorization and tamper-resistant records need deployment controls outside this local base. Source visibility is historical metadata, never permission to disclose. No public website or sending pipeline is included.
